The short version

A space shooter where nothing stops.

FUCK OFF is 2D space combat seen from above. You turn, you thrust, and your ship keeps whatever speed you gave it until you thrust the other way. Guns, bombs, mines, rockets and a few dirty tricks all draw on one energy bar, and that bar is also your health.

The flight model is rebuilt from measurements of SubSpace Continuum, the online space shooter from the late 1990s, and it runs at 100 ticks a second. If you flew there, your hands will know it. If you didn't, the tutorial will teach you, loudly.

FUCK OFF is an independent game by 7 Ants Games. It is not affiliated with SubSpace or Continuum.

Ways to play

  • The pub. Two teams, humans and bot pilots, fight over the orbs in an arena. Hold every orb for three minutes to win the map.
  • Free for all. The Drift: open space, rocks, no sides and no orbs.
  • Team matches. Timed, fixed sides, started from a lobby.
  • Duel a bot. One on one, offline, right in the page.
  • The tutorial. Eleven lessons and a boss. Offline too.

Start in a minute

  1. Open play.7antsgames.com in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a desktop or laptop. You need a keyboard.
  2. New to this kind of flying? Take the tutorial first.
  3. Press Play online to join the hosted zone, or duel a bot until you stop hitting walls.

The fleet

Eight ships. Pick your problem.

Ships 1 to 4 fly for Team 1, ships 5 to 8 for Team 2. Change ship, and side, whenever the pub lets you.

Bars compare the eight ships. Fuller and greener is better, so on costs and reload, less fills the bar.

The pub

Hold every orb.
For three whole minutes.

Two sides fight over the orbs inside the arena. Fly over one to take it for your side. Hold all of them for three minutes and the pub is won, and the next match starts on the next map.

The pilots are bots, and they are characters: each has a name, a personality, a mood, memories, grudges and a skill that grows. They chat in their own voice and remember who killed them. They are always marked as bots.

The maps

The Hollow

The default pub map. Two ways in: the Throat, short and narrow with doors, and the Dock, wide and straight with a gun gallery on either side.

The Compass

A station of four sectors, the Guns, the Hangar, the Warren and the Reliquary, that all open on the Hub in the middle.

The Bulwark

The Trench: three narrow lanes, and only the middle one goes through. The Twins: two wide tunnels on the far side.

The Drift

Free for all. Open space, rocks, no sides and no orbs. Every pilot for themselves.

The tutorial

Eleven lessons. Then it gets personal.

A narrator walks you through the ship one lesson at a time and has opinions about every wall you hit. Each lesson is short if you are competent. The last one is a fight with the tutorial itself: you need one kill, it needs five. Losing still counts as finishing.
