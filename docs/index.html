Mo' Mentum

Space has no brakes.
Neither do you.

A ship keeps its speed until it thrusts the other way. To stop, turn around and push. Every shot inherits your velocity, every wall bounces you, and every pilot in the arena runs on the same simulation.

The fleet

Eight ships. Pick your problem.

Ships 1 to 4 fly for Team 1, ships 5 to 8 for Team 2. Change ship, and side, whenever the pub lets you.

Bars compare the eight ships. Fuller and greener is better, so on costs and reload, less fills the bar.

The pub

Hold every orb.
For three whole minutes.

Two sides fight over the orbs inside the arena. Fly over one to take it for your side. Hold all of them for three minutes and the pub is won, and the next match starts on the next map.

The pilots are bots, and they are characters: each has a name, a personality, a mood, memories, grudges and a skill that grows. They chat in their own voice and remember who killed them. They are always marked as bots.

The maps

The Hollow

The default pub map. Two ways in: the Throat, short and narrow with doors, and the Dock, wide and straight with a gun gallery on either side.

The Compass

A station of four sectors, the Guns, the Hangar, the Warren and the Reliquary, that all open on the Hub in the middle.

The Bulwark

The Trench: three narrow lanes, and only the middle one goes through. The Twins: two wide tunnels on the far side.

The Drift

Free for all. Open space, rocks, no sides and no orbs. Every pilot for themselves.

Space has no brakes. Neither do you.

The tutorial fights back.

A narrator teaches you to fly, one lesson at a time, and roasts your flying the whole way. Then comes the final boss: the tutorial itself. You need one kill. It needs five.
