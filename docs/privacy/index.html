Privacy notice

What the FUCK OFF online zone and this website keep about you, why, for how long, and how to make it go away.

This notice covers the online zone of FUCK OFF (Mo' Mentum Mo' Problems): the game server, accounts, the game client while it is connected, and the browser version of the game at play.7antsgames.com. It also covers this website, 7antsgames.com (see the end). The game's emails and data folder still carry its working name, DSB Reborn. Offline play sends nothing anywhere (in the browser, loading the page is the only contact with our server).

Who runs it: Kennyd Sevenants (7 Ants Games), Belgium. Questions, requests and complaints: support@7antsgames.com.

The short version

  • To play online you need an account: a pilot name, an email address and a password.
  • Your email address is only used to send you codes (confirming the address, resetting the password) and to tell you about changes to your account. It is never shown in the game. There is no newsletter, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your password is never stored. The server keeps a one-way hash of it.
  • The game remembers how you play against its characters (kills, deaths, rivalries). That is part of the game, and it goes when your account goes.
  • You can delete your account yourself in the game at any time. Everything below that belongs to it is deleted at once.

What is stored, and why

Your account (on the server, in the account database)

  • Pilot name: other players and the characters know you by it. Kept until you delete the account.
  • Email address: for codes and account notices; one account per address. Kept until you delete the account.
  • Password hash (Argon2id): to check your password. Nobody can read your password from it, including us. Kept until you change the password or delete the account.
  • When the account was made, the last login, and whether the address is confirmed: for running the account. Kept until you delete the account.
  • Codes we email you (6 digits): for confirming the address and resetting the password. Stored only as a hash, valid for 30 minutes and 5 tries, gone when used.
  • "Stay logged in" tokens: to log you in without the password. Stored only as a hash. Each ends 30 days after you last used it, and all of them end when the password is reset or the account is deleted.

An account whose address was never confirmed is deleted automatically after 7 days.

To stop people guessing passwords, the server counts wrong passwords per account name or address for 15 minutes. This count is kept in memory only, never written to disk.

Backups

Every hour the server makes a copy of the account and character databases, encrypts it with a key that only we hold, and stores it with Cloudflare R2 in Western Europe. We keep every copy for two days, then one copy a day, and delete each copy after 30 days. When you delete your account it is gone from the server at once, and from the backups within 30 days, as the older copies expire. We use a backup only to restore the server after a failure; a restore never brings a deleted account back.

Your pilot's history (on the server, in the game's character database)

The zone is full of computer-controlled characters (always marked with a robot icon) that remember the pilots they meet. For each human pilot the server keeps:

  • your pilot name, when you were first and last seen, a skill rating, and lifetime kills and deaths;
  • for each character that has met you: how it feels about you, kills and deaths between the two of you, how often you met, and short memories such as "keeps getting destroyed by them".

This is how the characters can hold a grudge or remember a good fight. It is stored under your account's number, and deleting your account deletes all of it, including every character's memories of you.

While you play

  • Your connection: the server needs your IP address to talk to your computer. The game server does not log or store it. UpCloud, our hosting provider, may keep network records under its own policy.
  • In the browser: the page and the game connection come from our web gateway, which passes your game traffic to the game server on the same machine. The gateway, too, uses your IP address only to talk to your browser and never logs or stores it. It keeps no cookies and runs no analytics, .
  • What you do in an arena: your ship's movement and shots go through the server to the other pilots in that arena, and your name, ship and score are shown to them. The server keeps recent game state in memory only, so new arrivals can join the game in progress.
  • Chat: what you type goes to everyone in the arena, and characters may answer you. The server keeps the last lines of an arena's chat in memory only; chat is never written to disk.
  • Replays: the client saves a replay of each online game on the player's own computer, with the names of the pilots in it. Other players' replays of games you were in therefore contain your pilot name and how you flew. Those files are on their computers, not ours, and we cannot delete them.

On your own computer

The client keeps, in its data folder (on Windows %APPDATA%\DSB-Reborn\):

  • settings.txt: your settings, the last server address and name you used;
  • logins.txt: each zone's public key (to recognise the real server next time) and, if you chose "Stay logged in", your pilot name and a login token. Anyone with this file can log in as you until the token expires: keep it private. "Log out" removes the token from this file;
  • replays and the offline game's own characters, which never leave your computer.

The browser version keeps the same files (settings, logins, the last replay) in your browser's storage for our site (IndexedDB), not on our server. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them, including a "Stay logged in" token.

Who else sees your data

  • Resend sends the game's emails. It receives your email address, your pilot name and the message (for example a code). We send from Resend's EU (Ireland) region; Resend handles the mail under its own privacy policy.
  • UpCloud (UpCloud Ltd, Finland) runs the server the zone is on, in its New York data centre (USA). Your data is therefore stored outside the EU. UpCloud processes it for us under its data processing agreement, and protects transfers outside the EU with the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare R2) stores the encrypted backups. It cannot read them.
  • Other players see your pilot name, ship, score and chat while you play, and keep replays (see above). They never see your email address.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for advertising or analytics.

Security

In the browser, the whole connection is encrypted with TLS (HTTPS, WebTransport or a secure WebSocket); the certificate comes from Let's Encrypt. Account requests (passwords, codes, email addresses) are, in addition, encrypted between your client and the server with the server's key (X25519 and XChaCha20-Poly1305), and a recorded request cannot be replayed on another connection. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id. Codes and tokens are stored only as hashes. The client remembers each zone's key and refuses to log in to a server whose key has changed.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: pause menu, Delete my account, then your password. The account, its email address, its tokens and codes, and your pilot's history are deleted at once, and we email you to confirm.
  • Change your password: Forgot your password? on the login screen.
  • See, correct, export or restrict your data, object to how we use it, or anything else: write to support@7antsgames.com. We answer within one month. We may ask you to confirm the request from your account's email address.
  • You can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données, dataprotectionauthority.be) if you think we handle your data wrongly.

Legal basis, where the GDPR applies: running your account and the online game is the service you asked for (contract); counting wrong passwords and limiting requests protects the accounts (legitimate interest).

Children

You must be at least 16 to make an account.

This website

7antsgames.com is a set of plain pages: no cookies, no advertising, no tracking across sites, and fonts and the Slot Theory trailer served from the site itself. GitHub Pages hosts it, so GitHub receives your IP address to deliver the pages, under its own privacy statement.

We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service. Its script loads from gc.zgo.at, and each page view and a few clicks (the links to Steam, Google Play and the browser game, and pressing play on the trailer) are sent to GoatCounter. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It records the page, the referring site, your browser and operating system, screen size, country, and a one-way hash of your IP address and browser that changes every day and cannot be traced back to you or used to follow you across days. We use the counts to see which pages and links people use. Your browser's Do Not Track setting is honoured.

Links to Steam, Discord and the game take you to those sites and their own policies.

Changes

When this notice changes, its date above changes too. If we start using your email address for anything new, we tell you by email first.
