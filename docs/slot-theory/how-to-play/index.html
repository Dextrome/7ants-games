Guide · Your first runs

How to play Slot Theory

The whole game is one decision, made twenty times: which card do you keep? This is what each part of a run does, so you can spend your first runs on that decision instead of on the rules.

The run in one paragraph

A run is 20 waves on one lane. Before every wave you draft one card from five. You place what you drafted, start the wave, and watch: no building or drafting happens while enemies are on the lane. Every enemy that reaches the core costs lives. Clear wave 20, which ends with a boss, and you win. Run out of lives first and you lose.

The draft

You get five cards and keep one. What the five are depends on your board:

  • While you have a free slot, the draft aims for two towers and three modifiers.
  • Once all six slots hold a tower, every card is a modifier.
  • Once every tower is full of modifiers too, the draft offers small field mods for single towers, with a chance of rarer premium cards mixed in.

The game never offers a modifier that no tower of yours can take. Number keys 1 to 5 pick a card without the mouse.

Rerolls cost lives

Hate the whole offer? Reroll it. The price is lives, and it climbs every time you do it in the same run: 1, then 2, 3, 5, 8 and up. You always have to keep at least one life. Early rerolls are cheap; a habit of them is not.

Bans

Every run starts with five ban charges. Ban a card and it is swapped for another one right there, and it will not come back for the rest of the run. In Skirmish, once you have six or more towers unlocked, you can also ban up to three towers before the run starts.

Towers and modifiers

Towers sit on fixed slots on the map, six of them. You start with three towers available: the Rapid Shooter (fast, light shots), the Heavy Cannon (slow, heavy hits) and the Marker Tower, whose hits mark enemies so they take 40% more damage from everything for a few seconds. Eight more towers unlock as you clear campaign stages and maps.

Modifiers go on a tower you already own, up to three per tower. To assign one, pick the card, click the tower to preview its new range, then click the same tower again to confirm. Some modifiers to know first:

  • Exploit Weakness: a little more damage always, a lot more against marked enemies. Put it near a Marker Tower.
  • Chill Shot: hits slow enemies down for a few seconds.
  • Overreach and Hair Trigger: trade damage for range, or range for attack speed.
  • Focus Lens: a huge damage boost for a much slower attack.
  • Momentum: more damage with every hit on the same target.

Picks are permanent. You cannot sell or move a tower, and a run has no gold or economy, so the only resource you spend is the draft itself. One rare card, Decommission Order, is the single exception: it removes one tower and turns its modifiers into a damage bonus for the rest.

During a wave

Hands mostly off. Three things are still yours:

  • Targeting: click a tower to cycle what it shoots at: First (closest to the exit), Strongest, Lowest HP or Fastest.
  • Speed: the HUD button cycles 1x, 3x, 5x and 10x.
  • Your operator: each of the seven operators has a passive and a charged active ability that you aim yourself.

Towers also charge their own surge meter as their modifiers trigger. A full meter fires a surge whose effect depends on which modifiers the tower carries. Stacking copies of the same modifier on one tower fills it much faster.

Lives and leaks

You start with 25 lives on Easy, 20 on Normal and 15 on Hard. Most enemies cost one life when they reach the core. Heavier ones cost two or three.

The wave 20 boss

When wave 20 has finished spawning, a boss comes through the gate as the last enemy. If it reaches the core, the sector falls: you lose, however many lives you had left. The map decides which boss you face, and the map-select screen names it:

  • Breach Colossus: the one most maps send.
  • Breach Tribunal: its hull is sealed behind three nodes until an area effect, or three separate hits, opens it.
  • Breach Aegis: armoured to half damage until you slow it. Bring Chill Shot.

Where to go next

  1. Play the tutorial in the game. It is a scripted run that teaches the core systems.
  2. Start the first campaign, The Fracture Circuit. Its stages unlock new towers.
  3. When you want a fair fight with everyone else, try the Daily Rift: one seeded run a day, one attempt.
