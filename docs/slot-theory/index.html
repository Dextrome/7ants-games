A roguelike tower defense with a draft every wave.

Watch the trailer

Six hardpoints, twenty waves, one card at a time. Before every wave the game deals you five cards and you keep one: a tower for an empty slot, or a modifier that changes how a tower you already own fights. Then you let go. The wave runs on its own, and the build you committed to either holds the lane or leaks.

There is no shop, and nothing comes back. No selling, no moving, no gold to manage. Each run is a different hand, which is the roguelike part: you plan around what the draft gives you, not around a build you read online.

How a run works

Draft. Slot. Let go.

  1. DraftFive cards. While you have a free slot, expect towers and modifiers in the mix. Once all six slots are full, it is modifiers and upgrades.
  2. Slot itPlace a tower on an empty hardpoint, or put a modifier on a tower. Three modifiers per tower, and stacking the same one is a plan, not a waste.
  3. Hands offThe wave plays itself. You can change a tower's targeting, the game speed and your operator's charged ability. That's it.
  4. Survive 20Leaks cost lives. Wave 20 ends with a boss coming through the gate. If it reaches the core, the sector falls, however many lives you had left.
The full beginner's guide

Modes

Alone, together, or every day.

  • Campaign: The Fracture Circuit, The Null Protocol, The Terminal Convergence and The Rift Bloom. Mandates cap your towers, lock slots and buff the enemy. Clearing stages and maps unlocks new towers.
  • Skirmish: any unlocked map, any difficulty. On Hard, the Mandate Protocol lets you stack handicaps for a higher rating.
  • Endless: win wave 20 and keep going. A boss comes back every 20 waves.
  • Daily Rift: the same map, seed and rule twist for everyone, on Hard, one attempt per day, with its own leaderboard.
  • Co-op: two to four players hold one lane over Steam, each with their own slots and draft, sharing one pool of lives. Empty seats can take AI partners.
  • Custom Game, Map editor and Steam Workshop: build lanes and slots, add RIFT effects like warp pads and fog, playtest, and share them.
